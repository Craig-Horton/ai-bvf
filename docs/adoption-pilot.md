# AI BVF ten-team adoption pilot

Status: execution pack prepared; no teams have been enrolled by this document and no invitations have been sent. Use the [adoption plan](adoption-plan.md) for the programme priorities.

## Who the pilot is for

Recruit AI transformation consultants and internal AI portfolio leads who have one live investment decision to review within the next 30 days. Each team needs a decision owner and access to the person who can inspect the affected workflow.

Choose proposals where a team can return with evidence within 30 days. A second proposal is useful later; the first retention check is whether the team returns to the same decision and reviews what changed.

## Proposed success criteria

These are pilot thresholds for a cohort of ten enrolled teams, not current adoption results.

| Check | Target | Definition |
|---|---:|---|
| Enrolled teams | 10 | A named participant agrees to bring one live decision and attend the follow-up. |
| First completed assessment | 8 of 10 | The participant reaches a verdict, can explain its assumptions, and records one next action with an owner and review date. |
| Return use | 5 of 10 | Within 30 days of first assessment, the team reopens the same decision, updates evidence and completes a re-score. |
| Consented cases | 2 | The team explicitly approves a written account of the observed decision and what changed. |

Count each team once per measure. Also report returns among first completers, the number reminded, and the number who returned without a reminder.

A changed verdict is not required. A documented decision to retain a Stop or Fix can be useful when the team explains the evidence and next action.

## Session one, 30 minutes

Before the session, record the decision the participant would make using their current process. Ask them to withhold confidential names and sensitive details from any screen recording.

1. Let the participant open [aibvf.com/start](https://www.aibvf.com/start) and describe the proposal without coaching.
2. Observe where they hesitate, what they misunderstand and what they expect the assessment to return.
3. Have them explain the verdict, supplied versus estimated inputs, and the planning benefit range in their own words.
4. Ask them to select one evidence gap that matters to the decision, assign an owner and set a review date.
5. Have them save the decision and find the way back to it. Record any failure and whether assistance was needed.

Mark an assisted completion separately. A facilitator navigating the interface on behalf of the participant does not demonstrate independent activation.

## Exact interview prompts

Use these prompts in order, then follow the evidence the participant raises:

1. "What decision are you making, by when, and who can approve it?"
2. "How would you make that decision today without AI BVF?"
3. "What do you expect this assessment to give you?"
4. "Talk me through the result in your own words."
5. "Which inputs came from you, and which were estimated?"
6. "What do you understand the decision score and EUR range to mean?"
7. "What would you change, verify or stop because of this result?"
8. "Who owns that action, and what evidence will you bring to the next review?"
9. "What would make you return to this decision record?"
10. "What was missing, misleading or unnecessary?"

Record exact misunderstandings, including any interpretation of the score as a probability or the planning benefit as profit after costs. Those are product defects to address before expanding the pilot.

## Session two, 20 minutes

Ask the participant to recover the saved decision before offering help. Compare the original action and evidence with the updated record, and record whether the decision changed.

Use these prompts:

- "What evidence did the team produce after the first assessment?"
- "What changed in the proposal, work design or funding decision?"
- "What would you have done without the first result?"
- "Did you return because of a reminder or because the decision needed review?"
- "Which part would you keep using, and which part would you remove?"

Record claimed value separately from an observed change. A team's agreement to a case study does not prove financial impact.

## Review points

After the first three sessions, fix any blocker that prevents independent assessment or recovery of a saved decision. Re-test the same task with a participant who has not seen the fix.

At the end of the cohort, compare the four targets with the observed counts and interview evidence. If first completion is below eight or return use below five, identify the failed step and run another small cohort before expanding acquisition.
