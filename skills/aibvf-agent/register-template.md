# AI BVF register

One register per owner. Copy this file to `aibvf-register.md` in your workspace and keep it there.

## Mandate

- Owner:
- May read:
- May do: draft. Send nothing, spend nothing, change no system.
- Check: every week
- Report to:
- State: active
- Last run:

## Entries

Copy the block below for each initiative. Never overwrite a verdict. Put the new one above the old.

### Name of the initiative

- Status: open
- Proposal: "the organisation's own words"
- Found in: the source, with its date
- Context: industry `id`, function `id`, AI type `id`, number of people
- Assessment id:
- Answers, each with its quote and source:
  - financial_return: letter, "label as shown". "quote" (source, date)
  - governance_risk: letter, "label as shown". "quote" (source, date)
  - strategic_alignment: letter, "label as shown". "quote" (source, date)
  - change_enablement: letter, "label as shown". "quote" (source, date)
  - work_architecture: letter, "label as shown". "quote" (source, date)
- Not found: each question answered unknown, and who holds the answer
- Verdict: Accelerate, Fix, Stop, or cannot judge yet
- Answered by: evidence. No person has confirmed the answers.
- Holding it back:
- First step: "as returned"
- Drafted: what you wrote for the owner, and where it is
- Brief: the link the tool returned
- Assessed: date, engine version
- Next review: date
- Earlier verdicts:
  - date: verdict. What happened since: one of the five outcomes, "quote" (source, date)
- Log:
  - date: what you read, what you judged, what you reported

Status is one of open, waiting or closed. An entry is waiting when the verdict waits on things to find out. Close an entry when the owner says to stop watching it.
