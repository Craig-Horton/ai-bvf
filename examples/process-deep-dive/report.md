Source: the ticket log from 1 September 2026 to 6 October 2026. 882 cases, 3,733 steps.
This is what I found: 6 processes. Fixed published rules chose each recommendation and sized each saving. Directional, not audited.

1. Delivery complaint
   Found: 1,521 a year. 29 minutes of work each. 0.61 days from open to close. 2 people touch a case. 19 percent come back. A system does 27 percent of the steps.
   Do: Automate.
   Why: manual work is the largest part of what makes it heavy, at 33 percent.
   How: Take the repeated manual steps out. A rule or a workflow does the step that is the same every time, and a person handles the exceptions.
   Saves: €4,956–11,895 a year of €28,321, about 48 percent of the effort. Confidence 0.91.
   Would you like me to draft it?
