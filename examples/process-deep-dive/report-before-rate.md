AI BVF deep dive: customer service desk, Halden & Co

Source: the ticket log from 1 September 2026 to 6 October 2026. 882 cases, 3,733 steps.
This is what I found: 6 processes. Fixed published rules chose each recommendation and sized each saving. Directional, not audited.

1. Delivery complaint
   Found: 1,521 a year. 29 minutes of work each. 0.61 days from open to close. 2 people touch a case. 19 percent come back. A system does 27 percent of the steps.
   Do: Automate.
   Why: manual work is the largest part of what makes it heavy, at 33 percent.
   How: Take the repeated manual steps out. A rule or a workflow does the step that is the same every time, and a person handles the exceptions.
   Saves: about 48 percent of the effort. No euro figure yet: the hourly cost of this team is not in the log.
   Would you like me to draft it?

2. Supplier claim
   Found: 122 a year. 211 minutes of work each. 9.79 days from open to close. 5 people touch a case. 33 percent come back. A system does 5 percent of the steps.
   Do: Eliminate / insource.
   Why: rare and heavy. The rule: under 250 a year and heaviness of 50 or more.
   How: Stop running it as its own process. Fold it into the process next to it, or give it whole to the one team that owns the outcome.
   Saves: about 75 percent of the effort. No euro figure yet: the hourly cost of this team is not in the log.
   Would you like me to draft it?

3. Order status query
   Found: 3,042 a year. 6 minutes of work each. 0.08 days from open to close. 1 person touches a case. 2 percent come back. A system does 34 percent of the steps.
   Do: Automate.
   Why: short and repeated. The rule: 15 minutes of work or less, one person at most, 1000 a year or more.
   How: Take the repeated manual steps out. A rule or a workflow does the step that is the same every time, and a person handles the exceptions.
   Saves: about 48 percent of the effort. No euro figure yet: the hourly cost of this team is not in the log.
   Would you like me to draft it?

4. Refund request
   Found: 1,217 a year. 22 minutes of work each. 2.58 days from open to close. 3 people touch a case. 8 percent come back. A system does 34 percent of the steps.
   Do: Consolidate & re-sequence.
   Why: waiting is the largest part of what makes it heavy, at 29 percent.
   How: Redesign the flow before any AI. Cut the hand-offs: give one role the right to decide, and run the checks side by side.
   Saves: about 30 percent of the effort. No euro figure yet: the hourly cost of this team is not in the log.
   Would you like me to draft it?

5. Address change
   Found: 2,028 a year. 15 minutes of work each. 0.54 days from open to close. 1 person touches a case. 53 percent come back. A system does 70 percent of the steps.
   Do: Quality controls.
   Why: comes back. The rule: 30 percent of cases or more are reworked.
   How: Stop the error where it starts. Check the input when it arrives, so that the case does not come back.
   Saves: about 19 percent of the effort. No euro figure yet: the hourly cost of this team is not in the log.
   Would you like me to draft it?

6. Invoice copy request
   Found: 1,014 a year. 1 minute of work each. 0 days from open to close. Most cases are touched by nobody. 3 percent come back. A system does 92 percent of the steps.
   Do: leave it. It is light already, and a change would save too little to matter.

GenAI comes second. I recommend it for a process only after that process has been redesigned and judged again.
