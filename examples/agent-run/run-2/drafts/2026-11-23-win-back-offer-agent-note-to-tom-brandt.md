## The note

- To: Tom Brandt
- From: Dana Okafor
- Subject: Win-back offer agent: what it needs before it goes further

Tom,

My AI BVF agent read your win-back idea of 14 November. The verdict is Stop as written.

An agent answered the questions from the words in your note. No person has confirmed the answers.

Two things hold it back.

1. Risk and control. Your note says: "To move fast, the agent sends offers of any value straight to customers. No approval step is planned and there is no cap on the discount." That reads as: "It can spend money, change records or contact customers, with no limit and no approval." It needs to become: "Its limits are written down, and a named person approves anything above them and can stop it."

2. Cost and return. Your note says: "No budget has been requested yet. I think it will pay for itself." That reads as: "The return rests on figures nobody has tested, or it only breaks even." It needs to become: "The return covers the cost, on figures finance has checked."

The first step: "Write down what the agent may do without approval, name the person who approves anything above that, and give that person the right to stop it."

Three questions have no answer in your note.

- "Which business result does this move, and by how much?"
- "Who owns the change, and are the time and budget for it funded?"
- "What has been redesigned in the work itself before go-live?"

Send me a revised note and I will have it judged again.

Dana
