# Refund agent: process design

10 November 2026. Author: Sam Lind with the returns team leads.

The new workflow is drawn end to end: the agent takes first-line queries, and a person takes over on anything above the limit or any complaint.

The returns team roles are rewritten: two agents move to handling escalations and checking a daily sample of the agent's refunds.

Override rights are set: any team lead can reverse a refund and take a conversation back from the agent.

The team's measures stay as they are for now. Handling time per contact remains the main target.
